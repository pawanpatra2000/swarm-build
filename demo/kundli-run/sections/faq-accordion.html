Frequently Asked Questions

Vedic compatibility is an intricate science. Here are honest, precise answers to the most common inquiries regarding Kundli Matching and Guna Milan.

Is this service completely free?

Yes. Our online Kundli Matching and Guna Milan tool is fully accessible without any hidden charges, subscriptions, or premium paywalls. We believe that foundational Vedic calculations should be freely available to anyone seeking clarity and alignment in their personal life choices.

What if I do not know my exact birth time?

An exact birth time ensures the most precise calculations for the Ascendant (Lagna) and the subtle divisional charts. If you do not know the exact minute, you can input a close estimate. However, even small discrepancies can shift the Moon's nakshatra quarters or modify the Navamsha chart. If high accuracy is desired, we recommend consulting a professional astrologer for a birth time rectification process.

Is guna milan sufficient on its own for marriage?

No. While Guna Milan (Ashta Koota) is a highly structured and reliable initial screening system based on the Moon's positions, it represents only one dimension of compatibility. A complete Vedic matching process also evaluates the strength of the Seventh House (marriage), the current planetary periods (Dashas), individual longevity, and the presence of severe planetary afflictions like Manglik Dosha.

What is Nadi dosha and why does it matter?

Nadi represents the physiological and psychological constitution (classified into Adi, Madhya, and Antya, similar to the Ayurvedic humors of Vata, Pitta, and Kapha). A Nadi Dosha occurs when both partners share the same Nadi classification. In Vedic astrology, this is assigned the highest weight of 8 points because it traditionally indicates potential challenges in progeny, genetic compatibility, and long-term health harmony.
