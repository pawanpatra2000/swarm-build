Vedic Methodology

The Eight Kootas of Ashtakoota

Ashtakoota matching is a traditional, structured framework that analyzes compatibility across eight distinct dimensions. Each parameter carries a progressive weight, totaling 36 points.

The 36-Point Scale

The sum of all eight Kootas represents the total compatibility score of 36 Gunas. In Vedic tradition, a score of 18 or above indicates a balanced foundation for a harmonious partnership.

Maximum Score 36 Gunas
Minimum for Alignment 18 Gunas
Analytical Basis Lunar Constellations

Note: While a higher score indicates greater structural alignment, traditional astrologers also analyze individual planetary placements to provide a complete evaluation.

Koota 1 Max: 1 Point

Varna

Assesses work compatibility, spiritual temperament, and the natural skills and capacities of both partners.

Koota 2 Max: 2 Points

Vashya

Examines mutual dominance, emotional influence, and the power dynamic between partners to ensure balanced attraction.

Koota 3 Max: 3 Points

Tara

Analyzes birth star compatibility to indicate longevity, health, and mutual well-being throughout the partnership.

Koota 4 Max: 4 Points

Yoni

Evaluates physical compatibility, intimate bonding, and biological harmony based on animal archetypes associated with birth stars.

Koota 5 Max: 5 Points

Graha Maitri

Measures mental compatibility, intellectual friendship, and the natural alignment of the planetary rulers of both moon signs.

Koota 6 Max: 6 Points

Gana

Determines temperament compatibility, classifying individuals into Deva (divine), Manushya (human), or Rakshasa (demon) groups.

Koota 7 Max: 7 Points

Bhakoot

Analyzes emotional resonance, family welfare, and prosperity based on the relative position of the partners' zodiac signs.

Koota 8 Max: 8 Points

Nadi

Evaluates genetic compatibility, physiological balance, and the health of future offspring, carrying the highest weight in compatibility.
