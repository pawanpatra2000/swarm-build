Interpreting Your Compatibility Score

The Ashtakoota system evaluates compatibility out of a maximum of 36 points (Gunas). Understanding the mathematical thresholds helps put your compatibility report into traditional perspective.

Under Threshold

0 – 17 Points

Generally not recommended for marriage under traditional Vedic standards. Indicates significant friction in core temperament, mental alignment, or health parameters.

Guidance: Requires detailed chart review and specific remedies (Upayas) if proceeding.
Acceptable

18 – 24 Points

The baseline for a stable union. Indicates average, workable compatibility where mutual understanding, effort, and mature communication can successfully bridge natural differences.

Guidance: Recommended for alliance, provided major individual doshas are absent.
Very Good

25 – 32 Points

Strong emotional, mental, and physical compatibility. Indicates high harmony, shared values, and a natural supportive alignment that fosters long-term mutual growth.

Guidance: Highly recommended. Predicts a naturally cooperative and harmonious relationship.
Excellent

33 – 36 Points

An exceptional and rare alignment of core energies. Represents profound soul-level compatibility and near-perfect synchronization across almost all analytical parameters.

Guidance: Outstanding compatibility. Rare configuration indicating seamless life path alignment.

Critical Analytical Note: Dosha Exclusions

While the numerical Guna score is a vital baseline, it represents only one facet of Vedic matching. Critical factors like Manglik Dosha (Mars afflictions) and Nadi Dosha (physiological/genetic incompatibility) are evaluated independently. A severe, uncompensated Nadi Dosha or an unmatched Manglik configuration can override a high Guna score. Comprehensive matching always cross-references the numerical score with these primary planetary placements.
